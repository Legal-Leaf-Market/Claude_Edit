Read this before you automate 60 DMs Two separate risks, and the second one is the account-threatening one.
  • Sending the same three messages to dozens of accounts in one sitting is the exact pattern Instagram's spam heuristics watch for. The usual result is a temporary block on messaging, sometimes on the whole account.
  • Scripted sending (which is what the bookmarklet does) is against Instagram's terms, which prohibit automated interaction with the service. Your account is the thing at stake, so pace it: 10 to 15 people per session, spread across days, and vary the wording rather than firing three identical lines at everyone.

3. Your three quick replies, and the bookmarklet that sends them

Edit these, then drag the blue button to your bookmarks bar. The replies are baked into the bookmarklet, so re-drag it whenever you change them. Instagram cannot prefill a message from a link, which is why this has to type into the box for you.

Send 3 replies

How to use it: on an Instagram DM tab, click the bookmark once. A small panel appears in the top right and the hotkeys go live for that tab. Click "Send all 3" or press the hotkey. Repeat on the next tab.

  • Alt + Shift + I sends all three. This is the main binding.
  • Alt + Shift + 1 / 2 / 3 sends just that one reply.
  • Esc hides the panel. Clicking the bookmark again brings it back.
  • Ctrl + Shift + I is also wired to send all three, but Chrome claims it for DevTools and normally takes it first, so treat it as a spare rather than the one to reach for.
