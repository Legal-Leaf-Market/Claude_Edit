Follower outreach for legal_leaf_market

Two touches, not a blast. The intro is three short replies when someone new follows. The newsletter is one message about a week later carrying that week's pick. Anyone who says no is muted here and never appears in a batch again.

Worth keeping in mind
  • Firing the same message at dozens of accounts in one sitting is the pattern Instagram's spam heuristics watch for, and the usual result is a temporary messaging block. Ten to fifteen a session is the sane ceiling.
  • Scripted sending is against Instagram's terms, which prohibit automated interaction. Your account carries that risk.
  • An ounce under $50 is a US hemp offer. Most of this list is not in the US (Dubai, Tenerife, Thailand, Argentina, Trier, Barcelona, South Africa), so the pick is noise to them and possibly worse than noise. Mute rather than send.
  • One "no thanks" is the whole conversation. Mute them the moment it arrives, before the next batch.

1. Load your followers

The list starts empty and stays in this page's own storage, so a reload does not cost you the import. Screenshot your followers list on your phone, scrolling a screen at a time with a row of overlap so nothing falls in a gap, then drop the images here. Reading happens on your machine: the images are never uploaded anywhere.

Drop follower screenshots here, or click to pick them, or paste one with Ctrl + V.
Or paste handles as text

One per line, with or without the @. Anything after a comma, a dash or a tab on the same line is kept as the display name. Ending a handle with … or ... marks it as truncated, so it links to Instagram search rather than a profile URL that would 404.

Check each one before adding. A handle read wrong does not fail, it opens a DM with somebody unrelated, so anything the reader was unsure of starts unticked. Edit any cell to fix it.

3. Open the tabs

Chrome allows exactly one new tab per click, so this walks the list one at a time: click "Open next tab" or press O. Allow pop-ups for this page once (see below) and "Open batch" gives you the whole cap in one click.

How to get the whole batch per click instead of one

Click "Open batch" once. Chrome opens one tab, blocks the rest, and puts a blocked-pop-ups icon at the right hand end of the address bar. Click that icon, allow pop-ups and redirects for this page, then click "Open batch" again. The blocked ones were never lost, they are still queued. If Chrome will not keep that setting for a local file, the one-at-a-time path needs no setting at all.

Opening someone's tab stamps them as contacted for the current campaign, because that is the only signal this page gets. If you open a tab and do not actually send, click the state chip on their row to clear it.

4. This week's pick

One product, rotated each week, from legal-leafmarket.com. Use the /p/<id> share URL: that route emits the product's own Open Graph tags, so Instagram renders a real product card with the photo, name and price instead of the generic site card every other URL on the site produces. The name and price fields below are only for your own reference here, since the card carries them. UTM tags get added so this traffic lands in its own campaign rather than vanishing into direct.

5. The intro replies

Sent on first contact, three short messages. Unchanged from what you are already running.

6. Install the sender

Outreach sender

Both carry the current messages and this week's pick, so re-drag or re-download whenever you change them. On a DM tab, click the bookmark once: a panel appears top right and the hotkeys go live for that tab.

  • Alt + Shift + I sends the three intro replies.
  • Alt + Shift + N sends the one newsletter message.
  • Alt + Shift + 1 / 2 / 3 sends a single intro reply.
  • Esc hides the panel. Clicking the bookmark again brings it back.
  • Ctrl + Shift + I is wired to the intro too, but Chrome claims it for DevTools and normally takes it first.

A bookmarklet cannot arm itself, so it needs one click per tab. The userscript, in Violentmonkey or Tampermonkey, arms itself on every DM page instead, so each tab is just the hotkey and then Ctrl + W.
